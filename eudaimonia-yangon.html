Eudaimonia is coming to Yangon

Eudaimonia, the group's freight and supply chain company, is extending its service to Yangon — moving goods from origin to destination for businesses in and around the city.

Tell us what you need moved

We are taking early enquiries from Yangon businesses now. Send us the route, the goods and how often you ship, and the Eudaimonia team will contact you directly to arrange it.

  1. Send an enquiry using the form — it takes a minute.
  2. We contact you by phone or Viber to confirm the details.
  3. We move it — from your pickup point to the destination.
